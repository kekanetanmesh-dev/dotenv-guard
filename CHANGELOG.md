## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-10

### Added

- `check`: missing, empty, placeholder, extra, duplicate and syntax findings with exit codes, `--json` and `--github` annotations.
- `diff`: compare two env files by key without revealing values.
- `example`: generate `.env.example` from a real env file.
